Privacy policy

Last updated: [launch date]

This policy explains what information reduceturnover.ai collects when you visit this website or book a consult, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a consult, we ask for your name, work email, company and role, approximate headcount, and roughly how many people left in the last 12 months.
  • Usage data. We use privacy-friendly analytics to count page visits, the links that brought you here, and whether you clicked a booking button. We don't use this to identify you personally.
  • Cost model inputs. The numbers you enter in the cost model stay in your browser. We don't store them.

How we use it

  • To schedule and prepare for your consult, and to send you the one-page summary afterward.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To understand which pages and channels are useful, so we can improve the site.

We don't sell your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the site: a scheduling tool for bookings, an email provider, website hosting, and analytics. They process information only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep consult information for as long as it's useful for our working relationship, and delete it on request.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing max@reduceturnover.ai.

Contact

Questions about this policy: max@reduceturnover.ai.
