FAQ

Common questions

What data do you need from us?

At minimum, an anonymized export from your HRIS with hire dates, exit dates, role, team, and manager. Engagement surveys, exit interviews, and compensation data sharpen the analysis, but you don't need them to get started.

How do you handle employee privacy?

We work with de-identified data wherever possible, sign a DPA with every client, and only report results at a group level. Our models never flag individual employees to their managers.

How long does an engagement take?

A turnover diagnostic usually takes 4–6 weeks. Full retention programs run 6–12 months, with results tracked from the first quarter.

Do we need a large HR team to work with you?

No. Many of our clients have a small HR function. We do the heavy lifting and hand over tools and playbooks your team can keep running.

What does "AI" mean in what you do?

We use machine-learning models to spot patterns in your workforce data, such as which combinations of tenure, workload, and manager changes come before exits. People make the decisions. The models help you know where to look first.
